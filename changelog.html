What's new.

What has changed in each version.

0.2.1

October 2026

Big groups and licences

  • Walk-in registration for elections with no voter list: a desk registers people as they arrive and sends them to a station. The station can ask them to retype their student number or ID, and only a scrambled version of it is kept.
  • An installer for Windows, so COLection installs like any other program.
  • Licences can have a start date, can be copied from the program, and can be removed again (entering the same line brings it back).
  • Registering a walk-in voter respects the free version's voter limit.

0.2.0

October 2026

Seating plans

  • A new module for events: draw a floor plan with round, rectangular and banquet tables, walls, doorways and other elements.
  • Add guests by hand or from a spreadsheet, with groups, meal choices, dietary needs and RSVPs.
  • Say who should sit together or apart, and let Auto-arrange find the best seating. Lines on the plan show wishes met and missed.
  • Suggest swaps, snapshots with compare, and late changes: cancellations and plus-ones.
  • Find a guest from anywhere in the plan.
  • Print the floor plan, table lists, place cards and lookup sheets, with previews, your own logo and your choice of paper.
  • Snapping, rulers, distance labels and an alert when two tables overlap.
  • The free version allows up to 40 guests per plan for arranging and printing.

A new look

  • The whole program has moved to a new interface, with light, dark and match-this-computer appearance.
  • Opening New election, Voting station or Take over hosting now slides the form into the main window instead of opening a popup.
  • Each election can have its own icon on the home screen.

Step 5: Export results

  • A new step with every report setting in one place, and a live PDF preview that updates as you tick things.
  • Zoom the preview in and out, and make the text and pictures in the report bigger or smaller.
  • Un-ticking a heading, such as a position, greys out and un-ticks the choices under it.

Smaller fixes

  • The turnout bar no longer overlaps its heading on the Collect votes page.
  • The step icons are all the same size.
  • Switching between tabs while running a vote now slides smoothly without a faded strip at the edge.
