What it does.

Run the vote

Voters use a phone or a voting station in the room. A person at the desk sends each voter a ballot. It works without internet. Online voting from home is on its way.

Count it

Handles ranked votes, several seats and limits on groups. Every round is shown, so the result can be checked.
